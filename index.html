Terms & legal

  1. Relationship to Apple's warrantyThis warranty is offered directly by the seller and is separate from, and additional to, any warranty or AppleCare coverage provided by Apple.
  2. Your rights under the Australian Consumer LawNothing in this policy replaces or limits your rights under the Australian Consumer Law. Whether those rights extend beyond this policy depends on whether the sale is made in trade or commerce, as opposed to a genuine one-off private sale. Where the seller is regularly reselling goods for profit, the sale is generally treated as being in trade or commerce — in which case guarantees such as acceptable quality and matching description apply automatically and cannot be limited by this page or the printed warranty card, including who chooses the remedy for a major fault. For a genuine one-off private sale, most of those guarantees do not apply, though the buyer retains a baseline right to clear title, undisturbed possession, and the goods being free of any undisclosed debts or security interests.
